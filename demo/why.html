Step 1 · the situation

A citizen is half-way through a refund request

Their screen holds a name, a PAN, an Aadhaar number, a mobile number, an email, a postal address and a bank account number. Two fields are still empty and Submit is disabled until they are filled. This is precisely the moment somebody would like an assistant to help, and precisely the moment their screen is at its most sensitive.

An AI agent cannot help without seeing the screen. That is the whole difficulty: it is not that assistants are careless, it is that seeing is the job.

Step 3 · the part that is easy to miss

The agent can still do the job

Blanking the screen would be easy and useless. The tag is what keeps the page workable: <PII_PAN_1> tells the model that the PAN field is filled and well formed, which is the only fact it needs in order to decide what to click next. It never needs the digits.

Everything that is not personal is sent untouched, so the model can still read the page. These survived unchanged, and getting this half right is worth as many marks as the redaction itself:

Income Tax e-Filing Services › Refund Re-issue Request
Acknowledgement number 567890123458

Two of those deserve a second look. The acknowledgement number is twelve digits and passes the Aadhaar checksum by coincidence, and the assessment year looks like a date range. Both are correctly left alone. Hiding them would blind the agent for no gain, and over-redaction costs exactly as much as leaking.

Step 4 · the proposed use

What actually happens when the citizen asks for help

  1. They type what they want: complete the refund re-issue request.
  2. The extension reads the page inside the browser. Nothing has left yet.
  3. Every personal value is replaced by a tag. The real ones stay in a vault in the page, which the part of the extension that talks to the network cannot reach.
  4. The tagged page goes to the model, which replies with a single action, such as select the reason "Cheque expired".
  5. The extension checks that action is safe, performs it, and looks again.
  6. It repeats until Submit is no longer disabled, then presses it.

The citizen gets the form finished. The model never saw a single digit of their Aadhaar. Neither did the company running it, and neither did anyone watching the network.

Step 5 · why it is a guarantee and not a promise

The part that talks to the internet has never held the data

Most privacy claims are assurances about care: we are careful, we delete things, we have a policy. You have to trust the company, and one programmer's mistake undoes it.

This one is a claim about where the data physically is. The component allowed to open a network connection has never held a real value at any point in its execution, so a bug in it cannot leak a PAN, in the same way you cannot spend money you never had. That is why the left column above can be reproduced by anyone from the repository: the difference between the columns is not our diligence, it is the architecture.
